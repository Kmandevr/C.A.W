# Agent guide

Generated by `caw docs`; don't edit by hand. Full details: [engine](engine.md) (language) and [function library](library.md).

## Writing a module

- A `.caw` file: optional header between `---` lines (`caw: 1`, `kind: generator|effect|library|pattern`, `name`, `summary`, `mode: byte|float`, `rate`), then code. The last line is the output.
- Byte mode keeps the low 8 bits of the output (`v & 255`); float mode outputs -1..1. Effects read `in`.
- Values are 64-bit floats with JavaScript operators; bit operators work on 32-bit integers; `x/0` and `x%0` are 0.
- Time: `t`, `time` (s), `step` (16ths), `beat`, `bar`, `pos` (0..1 through the layer's loop), `bpm`, `sr`.
- Notes: when a layer plays a pattern, each note runs the generator as its own voice with `trig` (1 on the first sample), `gate` (1 while held), `note` (MIDI number), `freq` (Hz), `vel` (0..1) and `rel` (samples since release); `t` and `time` count from the note's start, and in byte mode `t` speeds up with the pitch (C4 = the formula rate). Write playable sounds with them, e.g. `saw(freq) * env.adsr(gate, 0.01, 0.2, 0.6, 0.1)`. Without a pattern they hold C4 for the whole loop.
- Patterns: a `.caw` file with `kind: pattern` (and optional `length: 16` or `length: 4 bars`), then one line per note: `at note len [vel] [extras]`. `at`/`len` are in 16ths (decimals or fractions such as 13/3 for off-grid and triplets), notes are names (`A2`, `F#3`) or numbers, `C3,E3,G3` is a chord, and extras are `prob 0.5`, `ratchet 3` and `slide`. Pick it as a layer's pattern to play that layer's generator.
- `let x = …`; `param name = 0.5 in 0..1 step 0.1 "tip"`, `param on1 = on`, `param w = saw in [sine, saw]` (read `w.saw`); `global name = …` (shared by the project); `export let env = …` (others read `module.env`); `fn name(a, b) = …`; `/// docs` above any of them; `// --- Header ---` groups controls.
- Check your work without listening: `caw check FILE.caw` and `caw render FILE.caw -o out.wav --bpm 120` (prints peak and RMS, flags SILENT or CLIPPING).

## Functions

- `sin(x)`: Sine of x (radians).
- `cos(x)`: Cosine of x (radians).
- `tan(x)`: Tangent of x (radians); 0 where the cosine is exactly 0.
- `abs(x)`: Absolute value.
- `floor(x)`: Largest whole number not above x.
- `ceil(x)`: Smallest whole number not below x.
- `round(x)`: Nearest whole number; halves round up (like JavaScript).
- `trunc(x)`: Drops the fraction (towards 0).
- `sqrt(x)`: Square root; 0 for x <= 0.
- `exp(x)`: e to the power x.
- `log(x)`: Natural logarithm; 0 for x <= 0.
- `tanh(x)`: Hyperbolic tangent: a smooth clipper.
- `sign(x)`: -1, 0 or 1.
- `fract(x)`: The part after the decimal point, 0..1 (also for negatives).
- `mtof(note)`: MIDI note number to frequency in Hz (A4 = 69 = 440 Hz).
- `min(a, b)`: The smaller of a and b.
- `max(a, b)`: The larger of a and b.
- `pow(x, y)`: x to the power y (exact for whole y; 0 when undefined).
- `clamp(x, lo, hi)`: Keeps x between lo and hi.
- `mix(a, b, amount)`: Blends from a (amount 0) to b (amount 1).
- `beats(n)`: Seconds in n beats at the project tempo, for delay times and envelopes.
- `popcount(x)`: How many bits are 1 in x (as 32 bits).
- `clz(x)`: Leading zero bits of x (32 for 0).
- `ctz(x)`: Trailing zero bits of x (32 for 0).
- `bitrev(x)`: Reverses the 32 bits of x.
- `bswap(x)`: Reverses the 4 bytes of x.
- `bits(x)`: The bits of x as a 32-bit float, read as an integer.
- `float(i)`: The 32-bit float whose bits are i (the reverse of bits).
- `rotl(x, n)`: Rotates the 32 bits of x left by n.
- `rotr(x, n)`: Rotates the 32 bits of x right by n.
- `hash(x)`: A repeatable 32-bit scramble of x; changes with the project seed.
- `noise(x?)`: White noise in -1..1 from t (or from x), the seed and the call's place in the code.
- `rand(x)`: A repeatable random number 0..1 for x (the same x gives the same value).
- `randi(x, n)`: A repeatable random whole number 0..n-1 for x.
- `chance(x, p)`: 1 with probability p for x, otherwise 0; repeatable.
- `sine(freq)`: Sine wave, -1..1.
- `saw(freq)`: Band-limited sawtooth, -1..1.
- `wt(table, freq, pos?, unison?, detune?)`: Wavetable oscillator: plays a table (table.saw, table.basic …), morphing through its frames with pos, with up to 16 detuned unison voices.
- `square(freq, width?)`: Band-limited square/pulse wave, -1..1.
- `tri(freq)`: Triangle wave, -1..1.
- `ad(trig, attack?, decay?)`: Attack-decay envelope 0..1, started each time trig rises above 0.
- `env.ar(trig, attack?, release?)`: Attack-release envelope 0..1, started each time trig rises (same as ad).
- `env.adsr(gate, attack, decay, sustain, release)`: Attack-decay-sustain-release envelope 0..1: holds at sustain while gate is above 0, releases when it falls.
- `slew(x, rise, fall?)`: Smooths x: moves toward it taking `rise` seconds up and `fall` seconds down.
- `lfo.sine(beats, phase?)`: Sine LFO -1..1 synced to the tempo.
- `lfo.tri(beats, phase?)`: Triangle LFO -1..1 synced to the tempo.
- `lfo.saw(beats, phase?)`: Rising saw LFO -1..1 synced to the tempo.
- `lfo.square(beats, phase?)`: Square LFO (1 then -1) synced to the tempo.
- `lfo.random(beats, phase?)`: A new random level -1..1 every cycle (stepped), synced to the tempo and the seed.
- `lpf(x, cutoff, q?)`: Low-pass state-variable filter (12 dB/octave).
- `hpf(x, cutoff, q?)`: High-pass state-variable filter (12 dB/octave).
- `bpf(x, cutoff, q?)`: Band-pass state-variable filter.
- `notch(x, cutoff, q?)`: Notch (band-reject) state-variable filter.
- `lp1(x, cutoff)`: Gentle one-pole low-pass (6 dB/octave).
- `hp1(x, cutoff)`: Gentle one-pole high-pass (6 dB/octave); removes rumble and DC.
- `fx.drive(x, amount)`: Warm saturation (tanh), level-matched so full scale stays full scale.
- `fx.crush(x, bits)`: Bit crusher: rounds the sound to fewer bits.
- `fx.fold(x, drive)`: Wavefolder: more drive folds the wave back on itself.
- `fx.delay(x, time, feedback?, mix?)`: Echo with feedback, up to 2 s (at 96 kHz).
- `fx.reverb(x, size?, damp?, mix?)`: Room reverb (mono Freeverb).
- `fx.comp(x, threshold?, ratio?, attack?, release?)`: Compressor: turns down whatever goes above the threshold.
- `glitch.hold(x, rate)`: Sample and hold: grabs a new value `rate` times a second and holds it.
- `glitch.stutter(x, on, length)`: While `on` is above 0, repeats the first `length` seconds heard after it rose (up to 1 s at 96 kHz).
- `glitch.mask(x, mask)`: Flips bits of the sound read as 16-bit numbers.
- `glitch.wrap(x, amount)`: Pushes the sound past full scale and wraps it around to the other side.
- `scale(degree, scale?, root?)`: The MIDI note of a scale degree; degrees past the end continue into the next octave.
- `quantize(note, scale?, root?)`: Moves a MIDI note to the nearest note of the scale.
- `chord(type, index, root?)`: The index-th note of a chord; indexes past the end climb octaves.
- `arp(type, step, mode?, root?, octaves?)`: Walks through a chord one note per step (up, down, up-down or random).
- `euclid(hits, steps, pos, rotate?)`: 1 on the steps where `hits` notes spread as evenly as possible over `steps`; otherwise 0.
- `drum.kick(trig, tune, decay)`: Kick drum: a sine whose pitch drops fast at the start of each hit.
- `drum.snare(trig, tone, decay)`: Snare: a short tone body plus filtered noise.
- `drum.hat(trig, open)`: Hi-hat: high-passed noise, short when closed, ringing when open.
- `drum.clap(trig, decay)`: Clap: band-passed noise with a soft attack.
- `synth.supersaw(freq, detune)`: Five detuned saws: a wide, bright lead or chord sound.
- `synth.pluck(trig, freq, decay)`: Plucked string: a bright attack that darkens as it fades.
- `synth.acid(gate, freq, cutoff, res)`: Acid bass: a resonant filtered saw that slides between notes.
- `synth.pad(freq, bright)`: Soft pad: a filtered supersaw.
- `synth.fm(freq, ratio, index)`: Two-operator FM: a sine whose pitch is wobbled by another sine.

- `util.gate(x, open)`: Rhythmic gate: lets x through only while open is above 0.
- `util.trem(x, length, depth)`: Tremolo synced to the tempo.

## Constants

- `scale.` major, minor, dorian, phrygian, lydian, mixolydian, locrian, harmonic, melodic, pentatonic, minorpenta, blues, chromatic, whole
- `chord.` maj, min, dim, aug, sus2, sus4, maj7, min7, dom7, maj9, min9, power
- `arp.` up, down, updown, random
- `table.` sine, tri, saw, square, basic, pwm
