# Woodpecker's recorded parts

`mallets.flac` holds parts of the **Versilian Community Sample Library** (VCSL) by Versilian Studios LLC, dedicated to
the public domain under [Creative Commons CC0 1.0](https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/) (source:
<https://github.com/sgossner/VCSL>, read 2026-11-24: the Marimba, Xylophone, Glockenspiel, Vibraphone,
Tubular Bells 1 and Timpani 1 folders). CC0 asks for no credit; it is given here with thanks.

Changes made for C.A.W: the first 174 ms (the timpani's 358 ms) of 152 strokes, trimmed, faded, folded to mono and
normalised per clip by `engine/tools/woodpecker_assets.py`, which also measures each key's modes from them. Woodpecker
plays them with its modelled bars, tubes and drums, so what you hear is not the original recordings alone. The
per-file manifest is in `docs/decisions.md` ("Woodpecker").
