# C.A.W brand and trademark policy

C.A.W's source code is open under the [Apache License 2.0](LICENSE). The licence covers the code only. As its section 6 says, it grants no permission to use the licensor's trade names, trademarks, service marks or product names. This page says how the brand may be used.

**Owner:** Kmandevr, doing business as AutoStacc ("AutoStacc"). AutoStacc reserves all rights in the brand below.

## The brand
- **Names and logos:** the names C.A.W, Code Auditioning Workspace and AutoStacc; the crow logo and app icon; the splash and startup art; and the rotating logo designs.
- **Product names:** the names of C.A.W's built-in instruments and effects (for example Raven, Talon, Roost and Murmur). The code's internal ids, such as `org.caw.compressor`, may be used freely for compatibility.
- **Mascot and theme art:** the crow mascot and its appearances in the app, the bird motifs in the device pages, and the named themes.

## What you may do
- Fork, build, change and share C.A.W under the Apache License 2.0.
- **Non-commercial use:** keep the brand in a fork you don't sell or monetise, as long as it says clearly that it is an unofficial build and not made or endorsed by AutoStacc.
- Say that your work is "based on C.A.W" or "compatible with C.A.W", in plain text.

## What needs permission
- Selling, or otherwise commercially distributing, anything under the brand, including a fork, a modified build, or a product whose name, logo or look suggests it is C.A.W or comes from AutoStacc.
- A commercial fork must remove the brand above and use its own name, logo and art.

## Content AutoStacc hosts
Sound packs, presets, kits and other content that AutoStacc distributes through its own services are AutoStacc's commercial products. They are not part of this repository and are not under the Apache licence.

AutoStacc may update this policy and may withdraw permission for a use that misleads people or harms the brand. To ask about a use, open an issue in this repository.
